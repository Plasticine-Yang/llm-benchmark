Lint notes from the screenshot tool and resolutions:
- The uppercase English labels (NOCTURNE ONE, SPATIAL ENGINE, PRIVATE LISTENING, RESERVATION CONFIRMED, SCENE labels) are intentional official product / instrument nomenclature, not duplicate or generic headings.

- "内容停在透明状态" in a section-only capture refers to below-the-fold reveal elements that have not yet entered the 88% threshold at that moment; scrolling plays the reveal once and the elements remain visible. Reduced-motion and full-scroll runs show all content.
- No remaining contrast, overflow or console errors were reported in the final runs.
